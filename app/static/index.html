    FAQ

    Good questions

    Why does 1080p and above take a little longer?

    YouTube stores high resolutions as separate video and audio streams. We download both at full quality and merge them with ffmpeg, which takes a few extra seconds but keeps every pixel.

    Is 320 kbps MP3 better than the original?

    No conversion can add detail that isn't in the source. YouTube's best audio is usually Opus at around 130–160 kbps. 320 kbps MP3 keeps as much of it as possible, and WAV or FLAC keep it without any further loss. Pick Original for the untouched stream.

    Which video format should I choose?

    MP4 plays everywhere. MKV gives the absolute best streams, including HDR, and is great for archiving. WebM is ideal for the web.

    Do you keep my files?

    Files live on the server only long enough for you to save them, then they're deleted automatically. Your recent lookups are stored only in your own browser.

    Can I download playlists or live streams?

    Not yet. Paste a single video. Live streams can be downloaded once they've ended.

    Is this legal?

    Download only content you own or have permission to use, and respect creators' rights and YouTube's Terms of Service. YTKit isn't affiliated with YouTube or Google.
